I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Virginia. My research lies at the intersection of visual navigation and control theory, with a focus on advancing multi-robot navigation systems.

I hold an M.S. in Applied Mathematics from Columbia University and a B.A. in Mathematics from Washington University in St. Louis. Prior to my Ph.D. studies, I spent three years as an Artificial Intelligence Engineer at MITRE, where I developed AI-driven solutions for complex engineering challenges.
